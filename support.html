Frequently Asked Questions

How do I enable notifications?

Go to the Account tab in the app, tap Notifications, and toggle on the ones you want.

How do I change my language?

Go to the Account tab, tap Language, and select your preferred language.

Is the app free?

Yes! Eluven is completely free. We offer optional one-time support payments to help keep the app running.

What Bible version does the app use?

Eluven uses the King James Version (KJV) which is in the public domain.
