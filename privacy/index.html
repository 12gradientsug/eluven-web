Your Privacy Matters

How Eluven protects your privacy while helping you read, understand and live God's Word.

◎ Last updated April 2026

At Eluven, your privacy matters. We are committed to protecting your information and keeping the app simple, safe and respectful.

Eluven helps you read, understand and live God's Word every day. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use and safeguard information when you use the app.

Information We Collect

We do not require you to create an account to use Eluven. We collect only minimal data necessary to provide the app's features, including device identifiers used to personalise your daily prayer and reading experience.

How We Use Your Data

Any data collected is used solely to improve your experience within the app. This may include remembering your reading progress, preferred language and whether you have opened your daily prayer.

“We do not sell your data to third parties.”

Local Storage

Eluven stores certain data locally on your device, such as cached Bible meanings and your daily prayer status. This data never leaves your device unless explicitly synced with our servers.

Third-Party Services

We use trusted services to support app features and content delivery. These services do not receive personally identifiable information from you unless clearly stated.

Push Notifications

If you enable push notifications, we may use your device token to send daily prayers and reminders. You can opt out at any time through the app's notification settings or your device settings.

Children's Privacy

Eluven is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes through the app. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your data, contact us at eluvenapp@gmail.com.
