Privacy Policy

Your Privacy Matters

At Eluven, we are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our app.

Information We Collect

We do not require you to create an account to use Eluven. We collect minimal data necessary to provide the app's features, including device identifiers used to personalise your daily prayer and reading experience.

How We Use Your Data

Any data collected is used solely to improve your experience within the app. This includes remembering your reading progress, preferred language, and whether you have opened your daily prayer. We do not sell your data to third parties.

Local Storage

Eluven stores certain data locally on your device, such as cached Bible meanings and your daily prayer status. This data never leaves your device unless explicitly synced with our servers.

Third-Party Services

We use trusted services to support app features and content delivery. These services do not receive personally identifiable information from you unless clearly stated.

Push Notifications

If you enable push notifications, we may use your device token to send daily prayers and reminders. You can opt out at any time through the app's notification settings or your device settings.

Children's Privacy

Eluven is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes through the app. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your data, please contact us at eluvenapp@gmail.com.
